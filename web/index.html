How it works

1 · ListenElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime turns live audio into text, sentence by sentence.
2 · ScoreGemini rates each passage −1 (dovish) to +1 (hawkish), compared with this speaker's own past answers.
3 · DecideA z-score of ±2 or more is a surprise. Markets were picked before the speech, so there's no search delay.
4 · TradeKalshi + Polymarket orders within $10/order, $50/day limits and a kill switch.
5 · ProveEach call is hashed onto Solana, stored in Tiger Data, and remembered by Backboard.
